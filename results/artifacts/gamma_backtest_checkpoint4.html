Reading this honestly

  • Buy & hold's much higher absolute return reflects a 15-year secular bull market it was fully exposed to; the strategy is in the market only ~32% of days by design. This chart is about whether the regime logic adds risk-adjusted value, not about beating buy & hold's raw return — that comparison is what checkpoint 5's regime-blind benchmark is actually built to answer.
  • Net Sharpe is essentially flat (≈0). Gross of the 2bps/turnover + financing cost assumption it's modestly positive — costs are consuming most of the edge at this trade frequency (~20 trades/year).
  • This is a single, fixed parameterization chosen from a diagnostic pass (checkpoint 4b: lookback and breakout-direction fixes), evaluated once on the whole sample — not yet walk-forward, not yet split into in-sample / validation / holdout, and not yet tested for statistical significance via HAC t-stats or bootstrap. That's checkpoint 5.
