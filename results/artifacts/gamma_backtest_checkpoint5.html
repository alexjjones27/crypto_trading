Reading this honestly

  • This is the full checkpoint-5 evaluation using the exact rules locked in after the leakage check (checkpoint 4b) — nothing tuned against these results.
  • "Regime-blind" runs the identical mean-reversion and vol-breakout entry/exit rules and vol-targeted sizing, but without the GEX regime gate deciding which sub-strategy is eligible each day. The paired block-bootstrap test on (strategy − regime-blind) daily returns is the direct test of whether the regime-switching logic itself adds value, separate from whether the underlying signals have any edge at all.
  • Buy & hold's much larger return reflects full exposure to a 15-year bull market the strategy is mostly not in (~32% of days); that's a feature of being risk-managed, not necessarily a fair like-for-like comparison on raw return alone — but it is a fair comparison on Sharpe, and buy & hold wins there too in every period.
