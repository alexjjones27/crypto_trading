Full sweep results

Flip count held at 3 in every single configuration across both sweeps — the caps only ever ration how much capital gets sized into winning trades, they never happened to gate a flip in or out of the realized path.

Reading this correctly. This sweep only asks how much of the bankroll the existing edge should be allowed to touch at once — it doesn’t change the edge itself, the flip‑rate belief, or the 3,364‑trade sample the whole strategy is built on. Every configuration here re‑runs the identical walk‑forward Beta(1, 300) flip‑rate prior over the identical trade sequence; only the capital‑allocation ceiling changes.
